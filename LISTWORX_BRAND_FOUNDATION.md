## 4. Five Core Values

Each value includes the plain-English meaning and the specific business practices that prove it. The practices listed are rules ListWorx already follows unless marked otherwise.

### 1. Honor God in Every Decision

**What it means:** We make decisions we could explain to God, our families, and our customers without embarrassment. Our faith shapes how we lead. It is not a slogan.

**How it shows up at ListWorx:**
- Before taking on a partner, product, or revenue source, we ask whether it protects our good name and treats people honestly (Proverbs 22:1).
- We turn down revenue that depends on misleading anyone, including ad copy that overstates what we deliver.
- We welcome and serve everyone with the same respect, whatever they believe.
- Faith is never a requirement or a scoring factor for contractors, customers, or partners. Contractors are evaluated on objective professional standards only.

### 2. Serve People Before Pursuing Sales

**What it means:** We start by asking what helps the person in front of us. When we serve people well, sales follow, and if they don't, we still did the right thing.

**How it shows up at ListWorx:**
- Requests are free for homeowners and realtors.
- A requestor's information is never sold, and contractors never see job or requestor details through ListWorx.
- Contractors can join Network free. A paid member who cancels keeps access through the paid-through date and then moves to Network free instead of being dropped.
- No per-lead fees, so contractors are never charged for a homeowner who never calls.
- People reach a real person at adeyo@listworx.co, not a ticket queue.
- If ListWorx isn't the right fit for a contractor right now, we tell them.

### 3. Choose Integrity Over Income

**What it means:** We would rather make less money than make it the wrong way. Trust takes years to build and one bad decision to lose.

**How it shows up at ListWorx:**
- Paid membership never buys referral priority. All three memberships receive equal referral consideration.
- IronClad Certification cannot be purchased. It is awarded and revoked only by an administrator.
- Payment never bypasses approval. A contractor who pays before approval is not activated.
- Referral counts are never fabricated or inflated. A referral counts once, when ListWorx delivers the contractor's contact information.
- We say exactly what IronClad Verified does and does not mean, including that ListWorx does not run background checks or verify coverage with insurers.
- We do not advertise a service until we know we can deliver it.

### 4. Help People and Businesses Grow

**What it means:** We are not here to collect fees from contractors. We are here to help good contractors become exceptional businesses, and to help homeowners and realtors make better decisions.

**How it shows up at ListWorx:**
- Network membership gives contractors access to the referral network at no cost.
- Contractors get practical tools today, such as the AI Toolkit for posts, follow-up emails, and review requests.
- We publish educational content that gives real, practical value, not filler written for search engines.
- We connect contractors with suppliers, education partners, and business-service partners as those relationships are confirmed.
- **[FUTURE]** Contractor Success Partner coaching, mentorship, and the Learn It to Earn It initiative.

### 5. Practice Responsible Stewardship and Generosity

**What it means:** Everything we have is entrusted to us. We manage money, data, time, and relationships carefully, and we use our success to help others.

**How it shows up at ListWorx:**
- We keep costs low and reinvest in what helps members and customers.
- We protect personal data: private document storage, privacy rules on referrals, and marketing email sent only to people who opted in.
- We keep commitments made to existing members, including earlier founding terms.
- We price fairly and plainly, with no hidden fees.
- **[APPROVAL NEEDED]** A specific generosity commitment. Examples of the kind of thing to decide: a set percentage of profit given away, a set number of community service days per year, or free memberships for veterans or new trades graduates. Do not publish any commitment until you have decided it and can keep it.

## 9. ListWorx Brand Voice Guide

This builds on the approved ListWorx Content Voice Standard (Nov 17 2026) and the tailgate voice already on record. It applies to the website, emails, blog articles, social posts, ads, and printed materials.

### Who we sound like

Two experienced business owners talking at a truck tailgate. One of them has been in the trades long enough to have seen everything, and he tells you the truth because he wants you to win. Straight talk, practical advice, a little humor, and no talking down to anyone.

### Voice traits

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Direct and conversational | Corporate, stiff, or salesy |
| Confident | Arrogant or boastful |
| Encouraging and practical | Hype or motivational fluff |
| Honest, including about limits | Vague, evasive, or exaggerated |
| Strong in our convictions | Attacking people or competitors by name |
| Clear about our faith | Preachy or performative |

### Writing rules

1. **No em dashes, and no dash-based separators.** Use a period, a comma, or a colon.
2. Plain English. Short words. Vary the sentence length. Use contractions.
3. Say one thing per sentence when it matters.
4. Give the reader something they can use: a step, a number, a question to ask.
5. Strong opinions are fine when we can back them up.
6. Never invent Alex's experiences, quotes, conversations, credentials, or customer stories.
7. Never promise guaranteed referrals, leads, rankings, revenue, or business success.
8. Never describe a proposed service as available (see Section 0).
9. Every draft is a draft until Alex approves the writing and the photos.

### Words and phrases

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| vetted, reviewed, approved | "background-checked," "fully verified" |
| referral, request | "leads" for what we deliver (we don't sell leads) |
| up to three approved contractors | "the best contractors," "top-rated pros" |
| IronClad Standards / IronClad Certified (only when awarded) | "certified" for someone who only agreed to the Standards |
| membership | "pay-to-play," "premium placement" |
| we're building, our goal is | "the #1," "the leading," "the most trusted" stated as fact |
| | elevate, unlock, seamless, game-changer, revolutionize, cutting-edge, robust, leverage, synergy |
| | "In today's fast-paced world," "Look no further," "Whether you're X or Y," "Let's dive in," "In conclusion" |

### How we talk about faith

- Talk about faith the way you'd talk about it with a neighbor: plainly, and without a sermon.
- Show it mostly through actions: honesty, fairness, generosity.
- Faith belongs on the About page, the Faith and Purpose page, the founder bio, and the footer line. It does not belong on checkout pages, pricing cards, or as a hook in ads.
- Never use faith to pressure a sale, and never hint that Christian contractors get better treatment.
- Scripture is used sparingly, quoted accurately, and the translation is named.
- Never speak negatively about people of other beliefs.

### Before and after

**Before:** "ListWorx revolutionizes the home services industry by seamlessly connecting you with top-rated, fully verified professionals you can trust!"

**After:** "Send one free request. We'll send you up to three approved local contractors. You decide who to call."

**Before:** "Upgrade to Growth to unlock premium visibility and supercharge your leads!"

**After:** "Growth doesn't buy you better placement. Nobody gets that. It gets you tools and support to win more of the work you're already referred."
